## CRITICAL BALANCE RULE

The slide must NOT be dominated entirely by the headline.

The hero title should attract attention first,
BUT the supporting content, diagrams, architecture insights, and takeaways must remain equally readable and visually meaningful.

Target balance:

* Hero title → 40%
* Supporting architecture content → 60%

Avoid:

* oversized headline consuming entire slide,
* tiny unreadable explanatory sections,
* excessive empty decorative space,
* content compression.

The viewer must understand BOTH:

1. the emotional hook,
2. the architectural lesson.

## TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

### Hero Headline

* Large and bold, but controlled.
* Maximum 3–5 words per block.
* Occupy no more than 35–40% of slide height.
* Editorial enterprise style.
* Strong but balanced presence.

Recommended size:
70–120 px.

### Supporting Insight Text

* Highly readable on mobile.
* Clear paragraph spacing.
* Strong contrast.
* Medium-bold emphasis for critical phrases.

Recommended size:
28–36 px.

### Section Headers

* Clearly prominent.
* Strong separation from body content.
* Avoid thin typography.

Recommended size:
36–48 px.

### Body Text

* Larger than traditional infographic text.
* Never visually secondary to the point of invisibility.
* Short architecture-focused statements only.

Recommended size:
24–32 px.

### Card Titles

* Immediate readability.
* Bold enterprise labeling.
* Minimal wording.

Recommended size:
28–36 px.

### Diagram Labels

* Must remain readable without zooming.
* Use fewer labels instead of shrinking typography.

Recommended size:
20–26 px.

## VISUAL HIERARCHY RULES

The eye flow should be:

1. Headline
2. Architecture problem visual
3. Failure propagation story
4. Architectural insight
5. Key takeaway

NOT:
Headline only.

## CONTENT DENSITY RULE

If the slide becomes crowded:

* reduce words,
* reduce decorative elements,
* simplify diagrams,
* increase whitespace.

NEVER reduce text readability.

## LAYOUT STRATEGY

Balanced editorial composition:

* left/right visual weight balance,
* headline balanced against architecture visual,
* diagrams large enough to teach,
* supporting content visible during fast scrolling.

Use:

* modular card sections,
* structured spacing rhythm,
* clean architecture blocks,
* clear operational narratives.

## DIAGRAM STRATEGY

Architecture diagrams should:

* explain failure propagation,
* show dependencies clearly,
* visualize assumptions/contracts/trust boundaries,
* remain simple and readable.

Avoid:

* tiny service labels,
* overloaded topology maps,
* dashboard-style clutter.

## COLOR SYSTEM

Use red ONLY for:

* primary failure,
* outage point,
* broken assumption,
* critical alert.

Everything else:

* calm blue,
* slate gray,
* warm white,
* subtle Azure.

## LINKEDIN MOBILE OPTIMIZATION

Every section must remain understandable:

* at 50% zoom,
* during quick scrolling,
* on mobile devices.

The slide should teach within 5 seconds.
