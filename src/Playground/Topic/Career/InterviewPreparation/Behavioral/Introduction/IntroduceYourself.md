# Important Interview Tips

## Avoid These Mistakes

### ❌ Do NOT:
- Tell your entire life story
- Mention every technology you touched
- Speak for 10 minutes
- Sound memorized
- Say “I know everything”

### ✅ Instead:
- Keep structure clean
- Speak confidently
- Show progression
- Show ownership
- Show architectural thinking
