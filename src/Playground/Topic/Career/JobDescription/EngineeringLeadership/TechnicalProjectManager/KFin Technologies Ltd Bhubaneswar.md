# Technical Project Manager

## About The Job

Technical Project Manager responsible for leading end-to-end delivery of FinTech platforms across payments, capital markets, wealth management, regulatory reporting, and enterprise data systems.

The role requires strong technical understanding, stakeholder management, architecture collaboration, risk governance, Agile delivery expertise, and experience managing large-scale financial systems.
