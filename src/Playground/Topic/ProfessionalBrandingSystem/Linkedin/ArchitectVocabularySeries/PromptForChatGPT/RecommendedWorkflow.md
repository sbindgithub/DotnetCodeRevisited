To turn your generated slides into a professional LinkedIn carousel, focus on three things:

1. Correct export format
2. Visual sequencing
3. LinkedIn upload optimization

LinkedIn carousel posts are simply PDFs uploaded as documents.

Recommended workflow:

1. Use [Canva](https://www.canva.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com) or [Figma](https://www.figma.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
2. Create all slides at 1080×1350 px
3. Export all slides as a single PDF
4. Upload the PDF to LinkedIn as a document post

Best professional workflow for your “🏛️ARCHITECT’S VOCABULARY” project:

Slide order:

* Slide 1 → Hook / Cover
* Slide 2 → Origin
* Slide 3 → Enterprise Meaning
* Slide 4 → Real-World Example
* Slide 5 → Business Impact
* Slide 6 → Story Panel
* Slide 7 → Architect Takeaway
* Slide 8 → CTA / Follow

Do not exceed 8–10 slides initially. LinkedIn retention drops fast after that.

Recommended CTA on final slide:

* “Follow for enterprise architecture vocabulary”
* “Save this post for architecture interviews”
* “Comment the next word”

Best export settings:

* PDF Standard
* High quality
* RGB color profile
* Keep each page portrait

In Canva:

1. Create Design → Custom Size → 1080 × 1350
2. Upload all generated images
3. One image per page
4. Align perfectly edge-to-edge
5. Download → PDF Standard

In Figma:

1. Create Frames 1080×1350
2. Place each image in sequence
3. Export frames as PDF

LinkedIn upload process:

1. Start post
2. Click “Document”
3. Upload PDF
4. Title example:
   “Architect’s Vocabulary — Event-Driven Architecture”
5. Write short high-retention caption

Good caption structure:

Hook:
“Most developers use this term incorrectly.”

Problem:
“Event-driven architecture is not just Kafka + queues.”

Value:
“This carousel explains the real enterprise meaning in under 60 seconds.”

CTA:
“Save this for system design discussions.”

LinkedIn algorithm optimization:

* Post between 8–10 AM or 6–9 PM IST
* Use 3–5 hashtags only
* First hour matters heavily
* Reply to every comment quickly
* Do not edit post immediately after publishing

Suggested hashtags:
#SoftwareArchitecture
#DotNet
#EnterpriseArchitecture
#SystemDesign
#SolutionArchitect

Critical quality rule:
Do NOT mix fonts, spacing, or alignment slide-to-slide. Consistency is what makes a carousel look enterprise-grade instead of “social media designed.”

For your positioning as an aspiring architect, this series can become a strong public portfolio if you publish consistently:

* 2 vocabulary carousels/week
* 1 architecture breakdown/week
* 1 system design case study/week

That compounds credibility over 6–12 months.
